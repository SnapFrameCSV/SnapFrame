Rules:
- Only OSI-approved permissive licences (MIT, BSD, Apache-2.0, ISC, 0BSD) or public-domain equivalents may be bundled into the extension.
- Copyleft (GPL/AGPL/LGPL) is not bundled. Weak copyleft (MPL-2.0) only as an unmodified dependency, never vendored.
- Fonts are not bundled; the extension uses the user's configured editor font at render time.
- Icons and images are original or CC0.
- Record the check date; re-check on every major version bump.
